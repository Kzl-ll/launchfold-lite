1Shipnote waitlist page — research notes, page skeleton, hero + pricing directions
1rResearch first: what to borrow, what to drop, what conflicts with the constraints

Note: the Modernist brand layer (color, type, components) is intentionally withheld here — these are structure-only wireframes. Brand gets applied once a hero and pricing direction are picked.

Patterns worth borrowing

  • One specific, outcome-driven headline + one field above the fold. The recurring shape across 2025–26 waitlist pages: outcome headline, one-line qualifier ("for indie SaaS teams"), email field, proof line — nothing else competing.
  • Trust above the fold, not in the footer. Pre-launch pages that convert put the credibility cue inside the hero block, next to the field.
  • Real product screenshot, not illustration. The screenshot is the primary trust signal pre-launch; the demo section carries most of the persuasion weight.
  • Adjacent proof instead of fake testimonials: live waitlist count, founder credential ("built by the person who shipped X"), integration/stack logos, "launching on Product Hunt [month]". Small honest numbers beat nothing.
  • Founder note as the proof section. With no customers, the human is the trust object — photo, 2–3 sentences, name/role.
  • CTA repeats at natural decision points, not on a fixed cadence: hero → after demo/how-it-works → after pricing → final CTA.
  • Objections as FAQ copy. Pre-launch FAQ is really risk-handling: when does it ship, what happens to my email, what if I hate it, is the price locked.

Patterns that read dated or hurt here

  • Nav-free "velvet rope" splash pages — fine for a one-field teaser, wrong for a page that has to carry pricing, FAQ and a founder note.
  • Three-tier pricing comparison pre-launch. Nothing to compare; it invites a decision the visitor can't make yet.
  • Uniform 3-column feature grids and feature dumping; bento grids as decoration.
  • Gradient-and-animation heroes, counters, marquee logo bars, scroll-triggered reveals.
  • Invented testimonials, fake avatar rows, "trusted by 10,000 teams" before launch.

Research that conflicts with your constraints — flagged, not silently dropped

  • Interactive/animated hero demo (the strongest 2026 hero pattern) needs JS. Substitute: one static annotated screenshot, plus an optional muted looping <video> with poster in the Demo section — no JS, degrades to an image.
  • Live waitlist counter can't be client-side. Substitute: server-rendered or hand-updated number in the proof line, dated ("412 signed up · updated weekly") so it can't look abandoned.
  • Conversational / multi-step capture and referral-on-confirmation loops are JS-dependent. Substitute: single email field posting to a real endpoint, referral ask on the server-rendered thank-you page.
  • FAQ accordion — use native <details>/<summary>. Zero JS, keyboard-accessible, degrades to fully expanded.
  • Sticky CTA bar on scroll conflicts with "one primary CTA in the viewport". Dropped; CTA repetition instead.

Tradeoff: the product is half-seen above the fold; relies on the crop to earn the scroll.
